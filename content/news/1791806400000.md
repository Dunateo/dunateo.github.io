<h2>Summary</h2>
<p>Patchstack reported active exploitation of CVE-2026-85097 (CVSS 10.0) in the Bricksforge WordPress plugin, which lets unauthenticated attackers upload and execute arbitrary PHP code on vulnerable sites.<br><br>Attackers targeted CVE-2026-102255 (CVSS 10.0), a pre-authentication server-side request forgery in SonicWall Secure Mobile Access 1000 appliances, with 135 exploitation attempts within days of its public disclosure.<br><br>ReliaQuest confirmed that the Qilin and Settra ransomware groups are actively exploiting a Palo Alto Networks VPN vulnerability patched in May.<br><br>The CEO of Cooperative U confirmed that a cyber-attack affected food stock in several stores of major French supermarket chains from October 11.<br><br>Japanese karaoke giant Daiichi Kosho said a supply chain breach exposed the personal data of more than eight million customers.<br><br>Researchers detailed an AI-augmented initial access campaign attributed to CyberXero against WordPress sites, e-commerce platforms and critical infrastructure in Ukraine, which combines Cobalt Strike with an orchestration layer powered by Anthropic&#x27;s Claude Code and stole more than 628,000 Ukrainian records.<br><br>Ledger paused sales through the reseller CryptoBilis after users in Southeast Asia reported stolen funds, with losses of more than $86, linked to devices bought there, and found an unauthorized hardware implant in one of the wallets.</p>
