<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/204136/security/air-force-cloud-one-breach-updates-blocked-migration-paused-scope-unknown.html'>Air Force Cloud One Breach: Updates Blocked, Migration Paused, Scope <span class='story__last'>Unknown</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--exploited' title='Exploited in the wild or zero-day: attackers already use it.'>Exploited</span> <span class='nb nb--vulnerability' title='A security flaw, its disclosure or its patch.'>Vulnerability</span> <span class='nb nb--breach' title='Data stolen, leaked or exposed.'>Data breach</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<p>Hackers breached Cloud One, a major US Air Force cloud computing platform running applications for personnel data, logistics, and military operations, by exploiting a software flaw to access its control plane. Discovered about a month ago during platform upgrades, the intrusion prompted officials to pause back-office application migrations, block application updates, and revoke administrator rights for some users. The scope of the breach, the identity of the attackers, and whether any data was stolen remain unknown, while US Cyber Command has been asked to assess the damage.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-2'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/204166/cyber-crime/booba-ransomware-attack-on-water-plant-reveals-monitoring-blind-spot.html'>Booba Ransomware Attack on Water Plant Reveals Monitoring Blind <span class='story__last'>Spot</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--ransomware' title='Ransomware attack, gang or extortion.'>Ransomware</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<p>Booba ransomware struck an East Coast water treatment plant in November and spread to the network of the local government overseeing it, though ThreatDown found no evidence that water operations were disrupted. Because the plant&#x27;s network was managed by another provider without ThreatDown&#x27;s software installed, attackers used an unmonitored machine to launch encryption before ThreatDown isolated 40 workstations and eight servers, saving 45 machines while three were encrypted. ThreatDown noted that Booba first appeared this summer and accounts for 44% of the ransomware compromises its analysts handled over the past 90 days, while SOCRadar counted 81 claimed attacks by November 17.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-3'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://www.theguardian.com/politics/2026/nov/27/dwp-cyber-incident-pensions-benefits-systems-offline'>DWP ‘cyber incident’ knocks almost all pensions and benefits systems <span class='story__last'>offline</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--breach' title='Data stolen, leaked or exposed.'>Data breach</span> <span class='story__source'>theguardian.com</span></p>
<p>The UK Department for Work and Pensions took almost all of its online pensions and benefits systems offline late Thursday after detecting unusual activity in a serious cyber incident. The outage prevented claimants from checking their state pension, applying for disability benefits, or claiming universal credit on Friday. The department urged people to stay alert for scams related to the disruption and to contact its helpline for urgent difficulties.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-4'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/suspected-vietnam-linked-hackers-target.html'>Suspected Vietnam-Linked Hackers Target Southeast Asia in South China Sea Espionage <span class='story__last'>Campaign</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--apt' title='Nation-state or advanced persistent threat actor.'>APT</span> <span class='story__source'>thehackernews.com</span></p>
<p>SOCRadar detailed a previously undocumented espionage campaign codenamed &#x27;WeaveLotus&#x27; attributed to a suspected Vietnam-sponsored hacking group. The threat actor targeted military, government, and legislative organizations across Southeast Asia to gather intelligence on South China Sea defense and military agreements, including five intrusions assessed in Thailand.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-5'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/204142/intelligence/fake-government-app-exposes-italys-expanding-spyware-surveillance-industry.html'>Fake Government App Exposes Italy’s Expanding Spyware Surveillance <span class='story__last'>Industry</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--malware' title='Malware family, campaign or tooling.'>Malware</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<p>An IrpiMedia investigation revealed that a fake Android app named FirmaDigitale, which displayed Italy&#x27;s Interior Ministry logo and promised asylum seekers updates on their protection status, delivered spyware named &#x27;Morpheus&#x27;. Building on a 2024 discovery by Lookout, the investigation attributed the malware to Italian surveillance supplier IPS, though it remains unknown who ordered the operation or whom it targeted, and police denied any involvement. The spyware can track phones, record screens, access files and messages, use the microphone, intercept internet calls, and hijack WhatsApp through a fake biometric prompt, with attackers also abusing telecom brands including Fastweb, TIM, Iliad, PosteMobile, and WindTre.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-6'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/pcloud-password-manager-hit-by-cyber.html'>pCloud Password Manager Hit by Cyber Attack Involving Email Address Exposure and Login <span class='story__last'>Attempts</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--breach' title='Data stolen, leaked or exposed.'>Data breach</span> <span class='story__source'>thehackernews.com</span></p>
<p>Swiss company pCloud confirmed that its cross-platform password manager, pCloud Pass, was targeted by a cyberattack involving login attempts and email address exposure after users reported unknown devices connected to their accounts. In a statement responding to posts on Reddit, the company said its team promptly contained the attack while noting that a small number of user email addresses were affected.</p>
</section>
