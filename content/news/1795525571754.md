<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/203865/cyber-crime/hackers-may-have-breached-fbi-database-used-by-police-nationwide.html'>Hackers May Have Breached FBI Database Used by Police <span class='story__last'>Nationwide</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--breach' title='Data stolen, leaked or exposed.'>Data breach</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<p>The FBI is investigating a possible breach of the National Crime Information Center (NCIC) database used by police nationwide for arrest warrants and criminal records. The database contained more than 18 million active records across 22 databases and served over 100,000 law enforcement organizations as of 2023. Officials learned of the intrusion in recent days, but the timing, method, and perpetrators remain unknown. President Trump and Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton have acknowledged the incident.</p>
