<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/new-n-able-4everable2shell-zero-day-exploited-hours-after-poc-released/'>New N-able &quot;4EverAble2Shell&quot; zero-day exploited hours after PoC <span class='story__last'>released</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--exploited' title='Exploited in the wild or zero-day: attackers already use it.'>Exploited</span> <span class='nb nb--vulnerability' title='A security flaw, its disclosure or its patch.'>Vulnerability</span> <span class='nb nb--malware' title='Malware family, campaign or tooling.'>Malware</span> <span class='story__source'>bleepingcomputer.com</span></p>
<p>Threat actors are actively exploiting a newly disclosed N-able N-central zero-day vulnerability dubbed &#x27;4EverAble2Shell&#x27; hours after a PoC was released. Attackers are using the flaw to compromise servers and deploy cryptocurrency miners. Researchers report at least 21 affected systems, including 19 running N-able&#x27;s latest security update.</p>
</section>

<section class='story' id='story-3'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/operation-endgame-takes-down-gootloader.html'>Operation Endgame Takes Down Gootloader, CastleRAT and StealC Servers, Seizes 6.4M Stolen <span class='story__last'>Logins</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--law' title='Arrests, takedowns, sanctions, regulation or government policy.'>Law &amp; policy</span> <span class='nb nb--malware' title='Malware family, campaign or tooling.'>Malware</span> <span class='nb nb--ransomware' title='Ransomware attack, gang or extortion.'>Ransomware</span> <span class='story__source'>thehackernews.com</span></p>
<p>An international police operation called Operation Endgame took down servers behind the Gootloader, CastleRAT, and StealC malware tools this week. Criminals used the three tools to access computers, steal passwords, and launch ransomware attacks. According to Germany&#x27;s Federal Criminal Police Office (BKA), police also seized about 6.4 million stolen logins belonging to more than 40,000 victims.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-4'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/silentair-hijacks-asian-telecom.html'>SilentAir Hijacks Asian Telecom Networks to Intercept SMS 2FA Codes and Spy on Taiwanese <span class='story__last'>Users</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--apt' title='Nation-state or advanced persistent threat actor.'>APT</span> <span class='story__source'>thehackernews.com</span></p>
<p>Stockholm-based telecom and cybersecurity company Enea identified a new spying group named &#x27;SilentAir&#x27; that used hacked equipment inside Asian mobile operators to intercept calls, texts, and SMS 2FA codes of selected people, mostly in Taiwan. Enea found the group&#x27;s attack sources in at least six Asian countries. The company assessed with high confidence that SilentAir is a state-level actor.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-5'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/202982/hacking/unpatched-check-point-gateways-are-being-wiped-exploiting-php2shell.html'>Unpatched Check Point Gateways Are Being Wiped exploiting <span class='story__last'>PHP2Shell</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--exploited' title='Exploited in the wild or zero-day: attackers already use it.'>Exploited</span> <span class='nb nb--vulnerability' title='A security flaw, its disclosure or its patch.'>Vulnerability</span> <span class='nb nb--malware' title='Malware family, campaign or tooling.'>Malware</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<ul class='story__cves'><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-109618'>CVE-2026-109618</a> <span data-severity='High'>High</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-109619'>CVE-2026-109619</a> <span data-severity='Critical'>Critical</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-123039'>CVE-2026-123039</a> <span data-severity='High'>High</span></li></ul>
<p>Check Point Research reported that unpatched Check Point Security Gateways were compromised in attacks starting on 30 October from the IP address 130.94.19[.]204, about a week after a security fix became available. Attackers exploited Mobile Access portals using PHP2Shell vulnerabilities CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 before escalating to root privileges via CVE-2026-123039 in the DynamicCLI IPC interface. In an initial wave hitting Israeli organizations, some victims suffered destructive wiper attacks, while a second wave on 2 November reached organizations in several countries without signs of destruction, with intruders deploying PHP webshells, the TSGA backdoor, and a network-monitoring tool called TSProxy.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-6'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/new-worm-like-sckit-malware-targets-msps-in-screenconnect-attacks/'>New worm-like &#x27;SCKit&#x27; malware targets MSPs in ScreenConnect <span class='story__last'>attacks</span></a></h2>
