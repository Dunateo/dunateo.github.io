<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/drupal-warns-of-new-highly-critical.html'>Drupal Warns of New Highly Critical Site Takeover Vulnerability Under Active <span class='story__last'>Exploitation</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--exploited' title='Exploited in the wild or zero-day: attackers already use it.'>Exploited</span> <span class='nb nb--vulnerability' title='A security flaw, its disclosure or its patch.'>Vulnerability</span> <span class='story__source'>thehackernews.com</span></p>
<ul class='story__cves'><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-136716'>CVE-2026-136716</a> <span data-severity='Medium'>Medium</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-132165'>CVE-2026-132165</a> <span data-severity='Medium'>Medium</span></li></ul>
<p>Drupal warned of a new highly critical site takeover vulnerability in Drupal core, tracked as CVE-2026-136716, that has been exploited in the wild. The flaw involves improper checking for unusual or exceptional conditions. According to Drupal, the issue relates to the CVE-2026-132165 Fail2Install vulnerability because it addresses remaining vulnerable installation states.</p>

<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--exploited' title='Exploited in the wild or zero-day: attackers already use it.'>Exploited</span> <span class='nb nb--vulnerability' title='A security flaw, its disclosure or its patch.'>Vulnerability</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<ul class='story__cves'><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2023-25914'>CVE-2023-25914</a> <span data-severity='High'>High</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2023-25915'>CVE-2023-25915</a> <span data-severity='High'>High</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2025-41450'>CVE-2025-41450</a> <span data-severity='High'>High</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-103406'>CVE-2026-103406</a> <span data-severity='Critical'>Critical</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-133275'>CVE-2026-133275</a> <span data-severity='Critical'>Critical</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-133303'>CVE-2026-133303</a> <span data-severity='Critical'>Critical</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-133311'>CVE-2026-133311</a> <span data-severity='Medium'>Medium</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-133323'>CVE-2026-133323</a> <span data-severity='High'>High</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-133324'>CVE-2026-133324</a> <span data-severity='Critical'>Critical</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-133469'>CVE-2026-133469</a> <span data-severity='Critical'>Critical</span></li></ul>
<p>The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) added vulnerabilities affecting Danfoss, Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) Juniper, Mitel MiCollab, and Vates Xen Orchestra to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. The additions include CVE-2023-25914 (CVSS 7.5), CVE-2023-25915 (CVSS 9.8), and CVE-2025-41450 (CVSS 8.2) in Danfoss AK-SM 800A and 8xxA Series refrigeration management systems, which can allow file reading, arbitrary command execution, or unauthorized web report generation. Also added were an OS command injection flaw in HPE Juniper Session Smart Conductor and Session Smart Router (CVE-2026-103406), Mitel MiCollab flaws (CVE-2026-133275, CVE-2026-133303, CVE-2026-133311, CVE-2026-133323, and CVE-2026-133324), and an input validation flaw in Vates Xen Orchestra (CVE-2026-133469).</p>

<section class='story' id='story-4'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/204004/hacking/switzerland-confirms-first-ai-agent-cyberattacks-on-local-organisations.html'>Switzerland Confirms First AI-Agent Cyberattacks on Local <span class='story__last'>Organisations</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--breach' title='Data stolen, leaked or exposed.'>Data breach</span> <span class='nb nb--malware' title='Malware family, campaign or tooling.'>Malware</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<p>Switzerland&#x27;s National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) confirmed its first reported cyberattacks carried out with the assistance of AI agents, compromising victims&#x27; IT systems including critical infrastructure operators across at least two sectors. According to the NCSC, attackers used dual-use Agentic Offensive Frameworks to target internet-connected systems, weak passwords, and known vulnerabilities while rapidly switching tactics to evade defenses. Noting that internet-accessible tools such as ARTEX and CyberStrikeAI have nearly doubled since October 2026, the agency urged organizations to patch internet-facing systems within 24 hours.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-5'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/204031/cyber-crime/1password-hacked-customer-names-and-emails-copied-vaults-untouched.html'>1Password Hacked: Customer Names and Emails Copied, Vaults <span class='story__last'>Untouched</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--breach' title='Data stolen, leaked or exposed.'>Data breach</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<p>On November 25, 1Password disclosed a backend breach in which an attacker compromised an employee&#x27;s corporate GitHub account to access source code and add malicious code that downloaded customer-related administrative data. The intrusion, first investigated on November 19, exposed names, sign-in email addresses, identifiers, account timestamps, public keys, Secure Remote Password parameters, and tokens for invitations, provisioning, and account recovery for a subset of customers, while customer vaults and secret keys remained unaffected. 1Password found no evidence that exposed tokens were misused and expanded its accompanying verification code from six numeric digits to ten alphanumeric digits.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-6'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/204049/data-breach/australias-flight-centre-data-breach-exposes-customer-records-and-passport-details.html'>Australia’s Flight Centre Data Breach Exposes Customer Records and Passport <span class='story__last'>Details</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--breach' title='Data stolen, leaked or exposed.'>Data breach</span> <span class='nb nb--supply-chain' title='Compromise of a vendor, package, dependency or CI pipeline.'>Supply chain</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<p>On November 26, Australia&#x27;s Flight Centre Travel Group disclosed a cyber incident involving a third-party accommodation booking system used by Flight Centre, Travel Associates, Cruiseabout, and some independent agents. An initial investigation found that most accessed records contained customer names and basic travel information, while a very small number included passport details, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, and rare instances of health or mobility information, with no evidence that payment card or bank account details were accessed. Flight Centre stated that flight bookings and corporate travel bookings were unaffected and that it is investigating with the supplier and Mandiant while notifying relevant authorities.</p>
</section>
