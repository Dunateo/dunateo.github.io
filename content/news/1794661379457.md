<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/203040/hacking/u-s-cisa-adds-check-point-and-unrealircd-flaws-to-its-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog.html'>U.S. CISA adds Check Point and UnrealIRCd flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities <span class='story__last'>catalog</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--exploited' title='Exploited in the wild or zero-day: attackers already use it.'>Exploited</span> <span class='nb nb--vulnerability' title='A security flaw, its disclosure or its patch.'>Vulnerability</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<ul class='story__cves'><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-114713'>CVE-2026-114713</a> <span data-severity='High'>High</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-123039'>CVE-2026-123039</a> <span data-severity='High'>High</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-109618'>CVE-2026-109618</a> <span data-severity='High'>High</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-109619'>CVE-2026-109619</a> <span data-severity='Critical'>Critical</span></li></ul>
<p>The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) added vulnerabilities in UnrealIRCd (CVE-2026-114713) and Check Point products (CVE-2026-123039) to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. CVE-2026-114713 has a CVSS score of 8.6 and enables command injection in UnrealIRCd versions from 6.0.0 up to, but not including, 6.2.7.1, with the maintainer confirming exploitation attempts shortly after disclosure. CVE-2026-123039 has a CVSS score of 7.8 and is being actively exploited alongside PHP2Shell flaws CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 to escalate to root privileges on Check Point gateways and deploy webshells, the TSGA backdoor, and the destroy_v6 wiper on VMware ESXi hosts.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-2'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/postgresql-patches-52-flaws-including.html'>PostgreSQL Patches 52 Flaws, Including 22-Year-Old Bug Enabling Authenticated <span class='story__last'>RCE</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--vulnerability' title='A security flaw, its disclosure or its patch.'>Vulnerability</span> <span class='story__source'>thehackernews.com</span></p>
<ul class='story__cves'><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-6460'>CVE-2026-6460</a> <span data-severity='High'>High</span></li></ul>
<p>PostgreSQL released updates patching 52 security flaws, including a 22-year-old type confusion bug tracked as CVE-2026-6460 with a CVSS score of 8.8. The vulnerability allows a database user who can create objects to execute code on the server. Full protection against CVE-2026-6460 also requires rebuilding any C-language extensions installed on the server.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-3'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/202997/cyber-crime/gootloader-malware-disrupted-after-infecting-25000-computers.html'>Gootloader Malware Disrupted After Infecting 25,000 <span class='story__last'>Computers</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--law' title='Arrests, takedowns, sanctions, regulation or government policy.'>Law &amp; policy</span> <span class='nb nb--malware' title='Malware family, campaign or tooling.'>Malware</span> <span class='nb nb--ransomware' title='Ransomware attack, gang or extortion.'>Ransomware</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<p>The U.S. Department of Justice and international law enforcement partners under Operation Endgame disrupted the Gootloader malware by removing it from more than 100 computer servers, including compromised WordPress sites, and seizing U.S. domain names. Since 2018, Gootloader has infected at least 25,000 computers worldwide and sold access to ransomware groups, leading to attacks on at least 44 U.S.-based victims and approximately $28.8 million in losses. Victims included the Department of War and the Defense Industrial Base, with the malware used to deliver threats such as SunCrypt and REvil ransomware, Kronos trojans, and Cobalt Strike.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-4'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/fbi-says-north-korean-hackers-stole.html'>FBI Says North Korean Hackers Stole Over $400 Million in Crypto Using Deepfake Video <span class='story__last'>Calls</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--apt' title='Nation-state or advanced persistent threat actor.'>APT</span> <span class='story__source'>thehackernews.com</span></p>
<p>The U.S. Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) established that North Korean threat actors stole more than $400 million in cryptocurrency over the last 18 months through a social engineering scheme codenamed &#x27;FaceStealer&#x27; that used deepfake video calls. The finding was shared as part of a coordinated set of actions announced by the U.S. government.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-5'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/new-mior-and-skyfall-attacks-let-rogue.html'>New Mior and Skyfall Attacks Let Rogue Cloud Hosts Steal Secrets From AMD SEV-SNP <span class='story__last'>VMs</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--vulnerability' title='A security flaw, its disclosure or its patch.'>Vulnerability</span> <span class='story__source'>thehackernews.com</span></p>
<p>Researchers disclosed two attacks named Mior and Skyfall that allow a rogue server host to read data inside AMD SEV-SNP-protected virtual machines. Both attacks require control of the server&#x27;s hypervisor, while Mior also requires control of the BIOS and can modify a VM&#x27;s memory as well as fake the signed report verifying what software it runs.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-6'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/trezor-confirms-hidden-hardware.html'>Trezor Confirms Hidden Implants in CryptoBilis-Sold Wallets That Can Steal Recovery <span class='story__last'>Phrases</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--supply-chain' title='Compromise of a vendor, package, dependency or CI pipeline.'>Supply chain</span> <span class='nb nb--malware' title='Malware family, campaign or tooling.'>Malware</span> <span class='story__source'>thehackernews.com</span></p>
<p>Hardware wallet maker Trezor confirmed that three Trezor Safe 5 devices sold by reseller CryptoBilis contained a hidden hardware implant consisting of an extra circuit board. The implant reads recovery phrases from the wallet&#x27;s screen and sends them over a mobile network to an attacker-controlled server. Trezor examined one of the compromised wallets in its lab.</p>
</section>
