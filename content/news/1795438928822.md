<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploit-critical-screenconnect.html'>Hackers Exploit Critical ScreenConnect Flaw to Hijack MSP Technician PCs Without <span class='story__last'>Warning</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--exploited' title='Exploited in the wild or zero-day: attackers already use it.'>Exploited</span> <span class='nb nb--vulnerability' title='A security flaw, its disclosure or its patch.'>Vulnerability</span> <span class='story__source'>thehackernews.com</span></p>
<ul class='story__cves'><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-84869'>CVE-2026-84869</a> <span data-severity='Critical'>Critical</span></li></ul>
<p>Threat actors are actively exploiting a now-patched critical vulnerability in ConnectWise ScreenConnect, tracked as CVE-2026-84869 with a CVSS score of 9.9, to target managed service provider (MSP) servers. The client flaw allows a guest to transfer and execute a malicious payload on a connecting technician&#x27;s host without warning.</p>

<section class='story' id='story-2'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploit-critical-aspnet-anti.html'>Hackers Exploit Critical ASP.NET Anti-CSRF Library Flaw Allowing Unauthenticated <span class='story__last'>RCE</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--exploited' title='Exploited in the wild or zero-day: attackers already use it.'>Exploited</span> <span class='nb nb--vulnerability' title='A security flaw, its disclosure or its patch.'>Vulnerability</span> <span class='story__source'>thehackernews.com</span></p>
<ul class='story__cves'><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-131914'>CVE-2026-131914</a> <span data-severity='Critical'>Critical</span></li></ul>
<p>Cybersecurity researchers have urged users to stop using the legacy ASP.NET anti-CSRF library Idunno.AntiCsrf following the discovery of a critical unauthenticated remote code execution vulnerability exploited in the wild. Tracked as CVE-2026-131914 with a CVSS score of 9.3 out of 10.0, the flaw affects versions 0.9.1.0 and 0.9.1 Beta of the library.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-3'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploited-glinet-cloud-flaw-to.html'>Hackers Exploited GL.iNet Cloud Flaw to Install Malware on Routers and KVM <span class='story__last'>Devices</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--exploited' title='Exploited in the wild or zero-day: attackers already use it.'>Exploited</span> <span class='nb nb--vulnerability' title='A security flaw, its disclosure or its patch.'>Vulnerability</span> <span class='nb nb--malware' title='Malware family, campaign or tooling.'>Malware</span> <span class='story__source'>thehackernews.com</span></p>
<p>Travel router and remote-access KVM hardware maker GL.iNet disclosed that hackers exploited a vulnerability in its GoodCloud remote management service to install malware on some customer routers and KVM devices. According to an updated security notice, the company identified and fixed the flaw on Friday. GL.iNet also blocked the access path used by the attacker so devices can no longer be infected through that route.</p>
</section>

<ul class='story__cves'><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-134624'>CVE-2026-134624</a> <span data-severity='Medium'>Medium</span></li></ul>
<p>N-able disclosed a critical server-side request forgery (SSRF) vulnerability uncovered during its investigation into the breach of its Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud email filtering service for managed service providers. <section class='story' id='story-5'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/203794/intelligence/bulgaria-discovers-massive-government-breach-after-five-years-of-undetected-spying.html'>Bulgaria Discovers Massive Government Breach After Five Years of Undetected <span class='story__last'>Spying</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--breach' title='Data stolen, leaked or exposed.'>Data breach</span> <span class='nb nb--malware' title='Malware family, campaign or tooling.'>Malware</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<p>Bulgaria uncovered a cyberespionage campaign running since 2021 in which attackers downloaded more than 130 GB, or about 62 million pages, of government documents including cabinet files, reports, and inter-ministry communications. The intrusion came to light after a new national cybersecurity monitoring system spotted suspicious activity in August, with no evidence so far that citizens&#x27; personal data was compromised. Investigators suspect several international hacking groups may be behind the breach but have not linked it to any specific country or nation-state actor. Authorities have isolated affected systems, blocked attacker access, removed the malware, and notified prosecutors.</p>
</section>

<section class='story' id='story-7'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/203767/malware/badbox-3-0-exposed-massive-botnet-hides-ad-fraud-inside-android-tv-boxes.html'>BADBOX 3.0 Exposed: Massive Botnet Hides Ad Fraud Inside Android TV <span class='story__last'>Boxes</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--malware' title='Malware family, campaign or tooling.'>Malware</span> <span class='nb nb--supply-chain' title='Compromise of a vendor, package, dependency or CI pipeline.'>Supply chain</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<p>HUMAN&#x27;s Satori Threat Intelligence and Research team uncovered BADBOX 3.0, a botnet infecting low-cost, off-brand Android Open Source Project (AOSP) TV boxes, smartphones, and tablets, some of which arrive with pre-installed backdoors. At its peak, the operation generated roughly 40 billion fraudulent bid requests per day, accounting for more than 4% of all connected TV traffic observed by HUMAN&#x27;s Defense Platform, more than three times the volume of VASTFLUX. The operators combined residential proxy network abuse with hidden-window video ad fraud that mimics legitimate connected TV applications and software development kits.</p>
</section>
