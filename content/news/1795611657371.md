<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/drupal-fail2install-exploit-details.html'>Drupal Fail2Install Exploit Details Emerge as Critical RCE Flaw Faces Active <span class='story__last'>Exploitation</span></a></h2>

<p>The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) added a critical remote code execution vulnerability in Drupal core to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on Tuesday due to active exploitation. Tracked as CVE-2026-132165 and dubbed &#x27;Fail2Install&#x27; with a CVSS score of 9.8, the flaw must be patched by federal agencies by November 27, 2026.</p>

<section class='story' id='story-2'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploited-cvss-100-hexpm-rce.html'>Hackers Exploited CVSS 10.0 Hex.pm RCE Flaw to Compromise 6 Package Registry <span class='story__last'>Servers</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--exploited' title='Exploited in the wild or zero-day: attackers already use it.'>Exploited</span> <span class='nb nb--vulnerability' title='A security flaw, its disclosure or its patch.'>Vulnerability</span> <span class='nb nb--supply-chain' title='Compromise of a vendor, package, dependency or CI pipeline.'>Supply chain</span> <span class='story__source'>thehackernews.com</span></p>
<p>An attacker exploited a CVSS 10.0 remote code execution flaw on Monday to run operating system commands on six production servers of Hex.pm, the package registry used by Elixir and Erlang developers. The vulnerability had existed in the registry&#x27;s code since February 2016, and maintainers deployed a patched build across all servers within about 15 minutes of the first malicious request.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-3'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/203946/hacking/gt13-ai-assisted-hackers-target-22-organizations-through-check-point-servers.html'>GT13: AI-Assisted Hackers Target 22 Organizations Through Check Point <span class='story__last'>Servers</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--exploited' title='Exploited in the wild or zero-day: attackers already use it.'>Exploited</span> <span class='nb nb--vulnerability' title='A security flaw, its disclosure or its patch.'>Vulnerability</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<ul class='story__cves'><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-61500'>CVE-2026-61500</a> <span data-severity='Critical'>Critical</span></li></ul>
<p>GreyNoise has detailed an unattributed, likely AI-assisted intrusion campaign tracked as GreyNoise Threat 13 (GT13) that targeted Check Point Security Management Servers and impacted at least 22 organizations globally between October 17 and November 2, 2026. The attackers operated from 99 IP addresses linked to ExpressVPN exit nodes and compromised Check Point devices, hitting victims across financial services, energy, government, transportation, mining, engineering, and manufacturing while generating over 1,400 network sessions. Researchers also observed the actor exploiting CVE-2026-61500 against a Rejetto HFS server to forge admin cookies, execute commands, and install an SSH key.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-4'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/203931/security/vmware-patches-critical-vcenter-authentication-bypass-and-esx-flaws.html'>VMware patches critical vCenter authentication bypass and ESX <span class='story__last'>flaws</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--vulnerability' title='A security flaw, its disclosure or its patch.'>Vulnerability</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<ul class='story__cves'><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-123903'>CVE-2026-123903</a> <span data-severity='Critical'>Critical</span> <span class='cvss' title='CVSS score quoted by the article'>CVSS 9.8</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-123890'>CVE-2026-123890</a> <span data-severity='High'>High</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-123910'>CVE-2026-123910</a> <span data-severity='TBD'>TBD</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-123911'>CVE-2026-123911</a> <span data-severity='Critical'>Critical</span> <span class='cvss' title='CVSS score quoted by the article'>CVSS 9.8</span></li><li><a href='https://vulnerability.circl.lu/vuln/CVE-2026-123912'>CVE-2026-123912</a> <span data-severity='Critical'>Critical</span> <span class='cvss' title='CVSS score quoted by the article'>CVSS 9.3</span></li></ul>
<p>Broadcom has released security updates under advisory VMSA-2026-0011 to patch 23 vulnerabilities across VMware products including vCenter, ESX, Workstation, Fusion, VMware Tools, and Avi Load Balancer. The fixes include four critical flaws, led by CVE-2026-123903 (CVSS score 9.8), an authentication bypass in vCenter&#x27;s VMware Directory Service that is distinct from the previously disclosed CVE-2026-123890. Broadcom also patched two CVSS 9.8 remote code execution flaws in the ESX I/O filter service, CVE-2026-123910 and CVE-2026-123911, along with CVE-2026-123912 (CVSS 9.3) affecting ESX, Workstation, and Fusion.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-5'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/203918/intelligence/maritime-industry-faces-growing-cyber-threats-as-50-ships-targeted.html'>Maritime industry faces growing cyber threats as 50 ships <span class='story__last'>targeted</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--exploited' title='Exploited in the wild or zero-day: attackers already use it.'>Exploited</span> <span class='nb nb--vulnerability' title='A security flaw, its disclosure or its patch.'>Vulnerability</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<p>United States authorities are investigating a wave of suspected cyberattacks targeting as many as 50 commercial ships carrying oil, liquefied petroleum gas, chemicals, and other potentially hazardous cargo, according to a Wall Street Journal report. Investigators are examining potential Iranian or Russian involvement, though a Trump administration official said no evidence has linked Iran to the incidents and described four attacks as minor disruptions. The incidents involved onboard network compromises exploiting Fortinet networking device vulnerabilities alongside bomb threats and ransom demands, prompting the US Coast Guard to deploy specialized cyber protection teams to at least four vessels.</p>
</section>

<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/203900/data-breach/eu-academy-users-names-and-emails-exposed-in-eu-commission-hack.html'>EU Academy Users’ Names and Emails Exposed in EU Commission <span class='story__last'>Hack</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--breach' title='Data stolen, leaked or exposed.'>Data breach</span> <span class='nb nb--apt' title='Nation-state or advanced persistent threat actor.'>APT</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<p>The European Commission confirmed on Monday that a cyberattack breached its EU Academy online learning platform and exposed users&#x27; names and email addresses. Access to EU Academy has been suspended, the European Data Protection Supervisor has been notified, and the Commission warned users of possible phishing follow-ups while the incident remains under investigation. CERT-EU detected the intrusion on 22 October and identified ARTEX, an AI-powered penetration-testing tool, as a possible tool used in the attack, with China-backed threat actors suspected.</p>
