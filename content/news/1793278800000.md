<h2>Summary</h2>
<p>Lumen&#x27;s Black Lotus Labs attributed Silent Train, a now-disrupted covert access network first identified in late 2023, to the China-nexus espionage group Volt Typhoon. Built from rotating chains of compromised SOHO, IoT and enterprise devices, it was used in four zero-day exploitation campaigns and for strategic access.<br><br>CISA added CVE-2026-41709, a Broadcom VMware ESX flaw, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog after CrowdStrike revealed that the China-nexus actor Warp Panda exploited it as a zero-day against the U.S. legal sector to reduce audit trail visibility.<br><br>Researchers detailed an apparently AI-assisted attack by a suspected China-nexus actor on October 25, 2026 that combined the PHP2Shell vulnerabilities with a kernel privilege escalation exploit to root 25 servers and breach a U.S. government-affiliated network.<br><br>Cisco Talos said hackers exploited a critical Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN flaw, fixed in February, to plant KeyDrift, custom malware inside SD-WAN controllers designed to copy and store the keys that encrypt traffic between sites. Talos describes the attacks as historical.<br><br>A critical flaw in UEFI firmware used by most server vendors and data centers lets attackers execute code early in the boot process.<br><br>A massive leak exposed records on 41,000 U.S. Homeland Security workers, including undercover ICE agents. It includes the locations of ICE and CBP facilities, workers&#x27; cell phone numbers and members of groups tied to wiretap programs, Palantir software and xAI&#x27;s Grok chatbot.<br><br>Cisco patched CVE-2026-76418 (CVSS 8.6), an actively exploited flaw in Secure Firewall ASA and FTD VPN software that lets an unauthenticated remote attacker crash the firewalls.<br><br>NebuSec disclosed a Telegram for Android flaw that, chained with two other bugs, gave an attacker with an ordinary account full control of a victim&#x27;s phone once the victim answered a voice call. Telegram fixed it in version 12.10.4 in late September.<br><br>Japan&#x27;s GMO Internet told customers to unplug their PCs in advance of a possible ransomware attack.<br><br>Alpine Linux said on October 28 that an attacker tried to exploit CVE-2026-97229 (CVSS 10.0) on its own GitLab server a week after GitLab released a fix. The flaw allows code execution through a crafted regular expression in a CI configuration; Alpine found no evidence the attack succeeded.</p>
