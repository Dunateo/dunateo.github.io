Patches are available; immediate updates are advised.<br><br>Major global shipping company 'Oceanic Logistics' suffered a significant cyberattack, likely ransomware, disrupting IT systems and port operations globally. It exfiltrates patient data before encrypting systems, exploiting unpatched VPN/RDP. Increased activity was noted over three weeks.<br><br>Regional healthcare provider 'MediCare Systems' was hit by a LockBit variant ransomware attack, causing service disruption and potential patient data exposure. Emergency services were diverted, and appointments canceled.<br><br>APT group 'FIN7' is actively targeting financial services in North America and Europe with custom malware and social engineering to exfiltrate financial data and intellectual property. Recent activity shows increased focus on investment firms.<br><br>'Palo Alto Networks Unit 42' detailed 'SpecterRAT', a new 'GhostRAT' variant. It features advanced anti-analysis and evasion via polymorphic code and legitimate Windows utilities, distributed via spear-phishing with malicious attachments.<br><br>A widespread, sophisticated phishing campaign targets Microsoft 365 users with convincing fake login pages and personalized email lures. It has compromised corporate accounts in finance and legal sectors.<br><br>CISA and NCSC issued a joint advisory on increased supply chain attacks, highlighting compromised software updates and poisoned open-source libraries.
