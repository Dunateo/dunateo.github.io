<section class='story' id='story-1'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/203309/data-breach/infoblox-confirms-supply-chain-breach-after-attackers-access-sensitive-data.html'>Infoblox Confirms Supply Chain Breach After Attackers Access Sensitive <span class='story__last'>Data</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--supply-chain' title='Compromise of a vendor, package, dependency or CI pipeline.'>Supply chain</span> <span class='nb nb--breach' title='Data stolen, leaked or exposed.'>Data breach</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<p>Infoblox confirmed on November 16 that attackers accessed internal company information through a compromised third-party data migration vendor. The supplier provided SharePoint migration services and held trusted access to Infoblox&#x27;s Microsoft SharePoint environment, which intruders abused to extract data. Stolen material includes employee personal information and customer Network Identity Operating System (NIOS) snapshots containing DNS and DHCP configurations, Grid databases, and reporting logs, though no passwords or login details were included. Infoblox stated it contained the incident and is investigating alongside outside cybersecurity experts.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-2'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hijacked-optimizely-scripts-served-card.html'>Hijacked Optimizely Scripts Served Card Skimmer and iOS Exploit Kits to CNN, BBC and Zoom <span class='story__last'>Visitors</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--exploited' title='Exploited in the wild or zero-day: attackers already use it.'>Exploited</span> <span class='nb nb--supply-chain' title='Compromise of a vendor, package, dependency or CI pipeline.'>Supply chain</span> <span class='nb nb--malware' title='Malware family, campaign or tooling.'>Malware</span> <span class='story__source'>thehackernews.com</span></p>
<p>Optimizely reported that an attacker exploited a flaw in Optimizely Web Experimentation to plant malicious code in scripts loaded by customers&#x27; websites. According to script-monitoring firm Report URI, visitors to websites operated by at least 178 organizations, including CNN, the BBC, and Zoom, received the hijacked scripts. The injected code was built to deliver a card skimmer and iOS exploit kits to site visitors.</p>
</section>
<section class='story' id='story-3'>
<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/203279/cyber-crime/germany-greiz-hospital-reopens-emergency-room-after-ransom-attack.html'>Germany: Greiz Hospital Reopens Emergency Room After Ransom <span class='story__last'>Attack</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--ransomware' title='Ransomware attack, gang or extortion.'>Ransomware</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<p>The Greiz-Ronneburg district hospital in Thuringia, Germany began gradually reopening its emergency room on Monday evening, 16 November, after a system failure on Saturday evening, 14 November forced the unit offline. District spokesperson Uwe Müller stated the incident was an extortion attempt and that an extortion message reached the hospital on Sunday. While patients continued to receive treatment and emergency cases were redirected via 112, several systems and digital treatment plans remain affected. State criminal police and prosecutors are investigating a suspect, and the hospital has not commented on whether patient data was compromised.</p>
</section>

<h2 class='story__title'><a href='https://securityaffairs.com/203260/hacking/google-gtig-warns-ai-agents-automate-exploits-steal-credentials-and-scale-cybercrime.html'>Google GTIG Warns AI Agents Automate Exploits, Steal Credentials and Scale <span class='story__last'>Cybercrime</span></a></h2>
<p class='story__tags'><span class='nb nb--malware' title='Malware family, campaign or tooling.'>Malware</span> <span class='nb nb--apt' title='Nation-state or advanced persistent threat actor.'>APT</span> <span class='story__source'>securityaffairs.com</span></p>
<p>Google&#x27;s Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) warned that attackers are deploying autonomous multi-agent AI frameworks such as ARTEX, CAIRN, CyberStrikeAI, and PentAGI to automate patch reverse engineering and execute multi-stage intrusions. GTIG observed CAIRN used in real attacks against targets including US aerospace organizations, while an operator used a modified version of PentAGI between late August and early October to scan organizations across Asia. Google also found evidence that a PRC-nexus espionage group is developing its own automated attack framework.</p>
