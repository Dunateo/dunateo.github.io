Hackers Exploit Critical ScreenConnect Flaw to Hijack MSP Technician PCs Without Warning

Exploited Vulnerability thehackernews.com

  • CVE-2026-84869 Critical

Threat actors are actively exploiting a now-patched critical vulnerability in ConnectWise ScreenConnect, tracked as CVE-2026-84869 with a CVSS score of 9.9, to target managed service provider (MSP) servers. The client flaw allows a guest to transfer and execute a malicious payload on a connecting technician's host without warning.

Hackers Exploit Critical ASP.NET Anti-CSRF Library Flaw Allowing Unauthenticated RCE

Exploited Vulnerability thehackernews.com

  • CVE-2026-131914 Critical

Cybersecurity researchers have urged users to stop using the legacy ASP.NET anti-CSRF library Idunno.AntiCsrf following the discovery of a critical unauthenticated remote code execution vulnerability exploited in the wild. Tracked as CVE-2026-131914 with a CVSS score of 9.3 out of 10.0, the flaw affects versions 0.9.1.0 and 0.9.1 Beta of the library.

Hackers Exploited GL.iNet Cloud Flaw to Install Malware on Routers and KVM Devices

Exploited Vulnerability Malware thehackernews.com

Travel router and remote-access KVM hardware maker GL.iNet disclosed that hackers exploited a vulnerability in its GoodCloud remote management service to install malware on some customer routers and KVM devices. According to an updated security notice, the company identified and fixed the flaw on Friday. GL.iNet also blocked the access path used by the attacker so devices can no longer be infected through that route.

N-able Discloses Critical Mail Assure Vulnerability Found During Breach Investigation

Vulnerability Data breach thehackernews.com

  • CVE-2026-134624 Medium

N-able disclosed a critical server-side request forgery (SSRF) vulnerability uncovered during its investigation into the breach of its Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud email filtering service for managed service providers. Tracked as CVE-2026-134624, the flaw allowed a user with high-level API access to open connections to internal systems of their choosing.

Bulgaria Discovers Massive Government Breach After Five Years of Undetected Spying

Data breach Malware securityaffairs.com

Bulgaria uncovered a cyberespionage campaign running since 2021 in which attackers downloaded more than 130 GB, or about 62 million pages, of government documents including cabinet files, reports, and inter-ministry communications. The intrusion came to light after a new national cybersecurity monitoring system spotted suspicious activity in August, with no evidence so far that citizens' personal data was compromised. Investigators suspect several international hacking groups may be behind the breach but have not linked it to any specific country or nation-state actor. Authorities have isolated affected systems, blocked attacker access, removed the malware, and notified prosecutors.

Cleverbridge warns hackers stole credit card numbers, CVVs in data breach

Data breach bleepingcomputer.com

E-commerce platform Cleverbridge is warning customers that hackers breached its systems and stole payment card information. The compromised data includes customers' credit card numbers and CVV security codes.

BADBOX 3.0 Exposed: Massive Botnet Hides Ad Fraud Inside Android TV Boxes

Malware Supply chain securityaffairs.com

HUMAN's Satori Threat Intelligence and Research team uncovered BADBOX 3.0, a botnet infecting low-cost, off-brand Android Open Source Project (AOSP) TV boxes, smartphones, and tablets, some of which arrive with pre-installed backdoors. At its peak, the operation generated roughly 40 billion fraudulent bid requests per day, accounting for more than 4% of all connected TV traffic observed by HUMAN's Defense Platform, more than three times the volume of VASTFLUX. The operators combined residential proxy network abuse with hidden-window video ad fraud that mimics legitimate connected TV applications and software development kits.

Hackers breach Austrian Foreign Ministry website, access diplomats' contact details

Data breach therecord.media

Austria's Foreign Ministry announced late last week that hackers breached one of its websites. The intruders accessed the names and official contact information of staff at its Vienna headquarters as well as at Austria's diplomatic missions abroad.

Spain Stops NoName057(16) From Getting a Physical Foothold

Law & policy securityaffairs.com

Spain's National Police arrested a man in Burgos last Tuesday suspected of leading what would have been the pro-Russian group NoName057(16)'s first physical cell in the country. Authorities held him on charges of belonging to a cyberterrorist organization and promoting terrorism after he allegedly spread propaganda, recruited supporters, and called on social media for cyberattacks against state institutions and confrontations with police. During a search of his home, officers seized crypto wallets, firearms, more than 5,000 rounds of ammunition, and other weapons.
