Drupal Fail2Install Exploit Details Emerge as Critical RCE Flaw Faces Active Exploitation

Exploited Vulnerability thehackernews.com

  • CVE-2026-132165 Critical CVSS 9.8

The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) added a critical remote code execution vulnerability in Drupal core to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on Tuesday due to active exploitation. Tracked as CVE-2026-132165 and dubbed 'Fail2Install' with a CVSS score of 9.8, the flaw must be patched by federal agencies by November 27, 2026.

Hackers Exploited CVSS 10.0 Hex.pm RCE Flaw to Compromise 6 Package Registry Servers

Exploited Vulnerability Supply chain thehackernews.com

An attacker exploited a CVSS 10.0 remote code execution flaw on Monday to run operating system commands on six production servers of Hex.pm, the package registry used by Elixir and Erlang developers. The vulnerability had existed in the registry's code since February 2016, and maintainers deployed a patched build across all servers within about 15 minutes of the first malicious request.

GT13: AI-Assisted Hackers Target 22 Organizations Through Check Point Servers

Exploited Vulnerability securityaffairs.com

  • CVE-2026-61500 Critical

GreyNoise has detailed an unattributed, likely AI-assisted intrusion campaign tracked as GreyNoise Threat 13 (GT13) that targeted Check Point Security Management Servers and impacted at least 22 organizations globally between October 17 and November 2, 2026. The attackers operated from 99 IP addresses linked to ExpressVPN exit nodes and compromised Check Point devices, hitting victims across financial services, energy, government, transportation, mining, engineering, and manufacturing while generating over 1,400 network sessions. Researchers also observed the actor exploiting CVE-2026-61500 against a Rejetto HFS server to forge admin cookies, execute commands, and install an SSH key.

VMware patches critical vCenter authentication bypass and ESX flaws

Vulnerability securityaffairs.com

  • CVE-2026-123903 Critical CVSS 9.8
  • CVE-2026-123890 High
  • CVE-2026-123910 TBD
  • CVE-2026-123911 Critical CVSS 9.8
  • CVE-2026-123912 Critical CVSS 9.3

Broadcom has released security updates under advisory VMSA-2026-0011 to patch 23 vulnerabilities across VMware products including vCenter, ESX, Workstation, Fusion, VMware Tools, and Avi Load Balancer. The fixes include four critical flaws, led by CVE-2026-123903 (CVSS score 9.8), an authentication bypass in vCenter's VMware Directory Service that is distinct from the previously disclosed CVE-2026-123890. Broadcom also patched two CVSS 9.8 remote code execution flaws in the ESX I/O filter service, CVE-2026-123910 and CVE-2026-123911, along with CVE-2026-123912 (CVSS 9.3) affecting ESX, Workstation, and Fusion.

Maritime industry faces growing cyber threats as 50 ships targeted

Exploited Vulnerability securityaffairs.com

United States authorities are investigating a wave of suspected cyberattacks targeting as many as 50 commercial ships carrying oil, liquefied petroleum gas, chemicals, and other potentially hazardous cargo, according to a Wall Street Journal report. Investigators are examining potential Iranian or Russian involvement, though a Trump administration official said no evidence has linked Iran to the incidents and described four attacks as minor disruptions. The incidents involved onboard network compromises exploiting Fortinet networking device vulnerabilities alongside bomb threats and ransom demands, prompting the US Coast Guard to deploy specialized cyber protection teams to at least four vessels.

EU Academy Users’ Names and Emails Exposed in EU Commission Hack

Data breach APT securityaffairs.com

The European Commission confirmed on Monday that a cyberattack breached its EU Academy online learning platform and exposed users' names and email addresses. Access to EU Academy has been suspended, the European Data Protection Supervisor has been notified, and the Commission warned users of possible phishing follow-ups while the incident remains under investigation. CERT-EU detected the intrusion on 22 October and identified ARTEX, an AI-powered penetration-testing tool, as a possible tool used in the attack, with China-backed threat actors suspected.

Treasury's FinCEN takes systems offline after cybersecurity incident

Data breach therecord.media

The Treasury Department's FinCEN has taken some systems offline following a cybersecurity incident. According to the department, the cyberattack resulted in unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems.

Colorado says cyberattack took down myColorado app, warns 150,000 had data stolen

Data breach therecord.media

Colorado authorities warned that cybercriminals likely stole data from 150,000 users of the myColorado mobile app. The cyberattack took down the application, which residents use for digital identification, document storage, and accessing state government services.

Radware discloses breach exposing customer cloud access keys

Data breach bleepingcomputer.com

Cybersecurity company Radware disclosed a data breach in which attackers accessed some customers' account details and cloud access keys. According to Radware, the intrusion occurred through an unsecured internal Cloud WAF (Web Application Firewall) management API that had been left exposed online.

Volant Manticore: Flying Under the Radar

APT Malware research.checkpoint.com

Check Point Research disclosed a previously undocumented Iran-linked espionage group dubbed 'Volant Manticore', whose activity has been traced back to September 2025 and overlaps in tooling and tradecraft with MuddyWater. The group initially focused on telecommunications companies, airlines, and a likely government target in Cyprus using phishing lures such as a fake CV to deliver the KuboShell backdoor and Homa Framework. In September 2026, Volant Manticore abruptly shifted its targeting to healthcare organizations in Saudi Arabia.
