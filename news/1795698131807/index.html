Drupal Warns of New Highly Critical Site Takeover Vulnerability Under Active Exploitation

Exploited Vulnerability thehackernews.com

  • CVE-2026-136716 Medium
  • CVE-2026-132165 Medium

Drupal warned of a new highly critical site takeover vulnerability in Drupal core, tracked as CVE-2026-136716, that has been exploited in the wild. The flaw involves improper checking for unusual or exceptional conditions. According to Drupal, the issue relates to the CVE-2026-132165 Fail2Install vulnerability because it addresses remaining vulnerable installation states.

U.S. CISA adds HPE, Danfoss, Mitel MiCollab, and Vates Xen Orchestra flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog

Exploited Vulnerability securityaffairs.com

  • CVE-2023-25914 High
  • CVE-2023-25915 High
  • CVE-2025-41450 High
  • CVE-2026-103406 Critical
  • CVE-2026-133275 Critical
  • CVE-2026-133303 Critical
  • CVE-2026-133311 Medium
  • CVE-2026-133323 High
  • CVE-2026-133324 Critical
  • CVE-2026-133469 Critical

The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) added vulnerabilities affecting Danfoss, Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) Juniper, Mitel MiCollab, and Vates Xen Orchestra to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. The additions include CVE-2023-25914 (CVSS 7.5), CVE-2023-25915 (CVSS 9.8), and CVE-2025-41450 (CVSS 8.2) in Danfoss AK-SM 800A and 8xxA Series refrigeration management systems, which can allow file reading, arbitrary command execution, or unauthorized web report generation. Also added were an OS command injection flaw in HPE Juniper Session Smart Conductor and Session Smart Router (CVE-2026-103406), Mitel MiCollab flaws (CVE-2026-133275, CVE-2026-133303, CVE-2026-133311, CVE-2026-133323, and CVE-2026-133324), and an input validation flaw in Vates Xen Orchestra (CVE-2026-133469).

Palo Alto Networks warns of Fragnesia flaw exploited in PAN-OS attacks

Exploited Vulnerability bleepingcomputer.com

Palo Alto Networks warned that the Fragnesia vulnerability is being exploited in attacks targeting PAN-OS. Threat actors are chaining exploits for the PHP2Shell and Fragnesia vulnerabilities to target Palo Alto Networks devices with management interfaces exposed to the internet.

Switzerland Confirms First AI-Agent Cyberattacks on Local Organisations

Data breach Malware securityaffairs.com

Switzerland's National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) confirmed its first reported cyberattacks carried out with the assistance of AI agents, compromising victims' IT systems including critical infrastructure operators across at least two sectors. According to the NCSC, attackers used dual-use Agentic Offensive Frameworks to target internet-connected systems, weak passwords, and known vulnerabilities while rapidly switching tactics to evade defenses. Noting that internet-accessible tools such as ARTEX and CyberStrikeAI have nearly doubled since October 2026, the agency urged organizations to patch internet-facing systems within 24 hours.

1Password Hacked: Customer Names and Emails Copied, Vaults Untouched

Data breach securityaffairs.com

On November 25, 1Password disclosed a backend breach in which an attacker compromised an employee's corporate GitHub account to access source code and add malicious code that downloaded customer-related administrative data. The intrusion, first investigated on November 19, exposed names, sign-in email addresses, identifiers, account timestamps, public keys, Secure Remote Password parameters, and tokens for invitations, provisioning, and account recovery for a subset of customers, while customer vaults and secret keys remained unaffected. 1Password found no evidence that exposed tokens were misused and expanded its accompanying verification code from six numeric digits to ten alphanumeric digits.

Australia’s Flight Centre Data Breach Exposes Customer Records and Passport Details

Data breach Supply chain securityaffairs.com

On November 26, Australia's Flight Centre Travel Group disclosed a cyber incident involving a third-party accommodation booking system used by Flight Centre, Travel Associates, Cruiseabout, and some independent agents. An initial investigation found that most accessed records contained customer names and basic travel information, while a very small number included passport details, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, and rare instances of health or mobility information, with no evidence that payment card or bank account details were accessed. Flight Centre stated that flight bookings and corporate travel bookings were unaffected and that it is investigating with the supplier and Mandiant while notifying relevant authorities.

Microsoft: China’s Salt Typhoon hackers are using Artex

APT Malware therecord.media

Microsoft reported that China's Salt Typhoon hackers and threat groups from North Korea are rapidly integrating the AI tool Artex into their hacking suites. The tool has already been implicated in several high-profile breaches.

13 Malicious NuGet Packages Turn Fake .NET Job Assessments Into Malware Delivery Traps

Supply chain Malware thehackernews.com

Cybersecurity researchers discovered 13 malicious NuGet packages designed to target Windows .NET developers under the guise of fake take-home job assessments. According to Socket security researcher Kush Pandya, most of the packages pose as WinUI 3 and Windows Presentation Foundation button controls to act as malware delivery traps.

SYS010 malware abuses browser management to spy on Gmail, Outlook

Malware bleepingcomputer.com

A new version of the SYS01 infostealer malware, referenced in the headline as SYS010, is being delivered through Meta ads to spy on Gmail and Outlook accounts. The malware enrolls browsers into enterprise-style management to force the installation of an extension that steals sensitive data, including Gmail and Outlook emails.
