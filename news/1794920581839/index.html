Infoblox Confirms Supply Chain Breach After Attackers Access Sensitive Data

Supply chain Data breach securityaffairs.com

Infoblox confirmed on November 16 that attackers accessed internal company information through a compromised third-party data migration vendor. The supplier provided SharePoint migration services and held trusted access to Infoblox's Microsoft SharePoint environment, which intruders abused to extract data. Stolen material includes employee personal information and customer Network Identity Operating System (NIOS) snapshots containing DNS and DHCP configurations, Grid databases, and reporting logs, though no passwords or login details were included. Infoblox stated it contained the incident and is investigating alongside outside cybersecurity experts.

Hijacked Optimizely Scripts Served Card Skimmer and iOS Exploit Kits to CNN, BBC and Zoom Visitors

Exploited Supply chain Malware thehackernews.com

Optimizely reported that an attacker exploited a flaw in Optimizely Web Experimentation to plant malicious code in scripts loaded by customers' websites. According to script-monitoring firm Report URI, visitors to websites operated by at least 178 organizations, including CNN, the BBC, and Zoom, received the hijacked scripts. The injected code was built to deliver a card skimmer and iOS exploit kits to site visitors.

Germany: Greiz Hospital Reopens Emergency Room After Ransom Attack

Ransomware securityaffairs.com

The Greiz-Ronneburg district hospital in Thuringia, Germany began gradually reopening its emergency room on Monday evening, 16 November, after a system failure on Saturday evening, 14 November forced the unit offline. District spokesperson Uwe Müller stated the incident was an extortion attempt and that an extortion message reached the hospital on Sunday. While patients continued to receive treatment and emergency cases were redirected via 112, several systems and digital treatment plans remain affected. State criminal police and prosecutors are investigating a suspect, and the hospital has not commented on whether patient data was compromised.

Uber notifies drivers of data breach affecting nearly 150,000 people

Data breach Supply chain bleepingcomputer.com

Ride-hailing company Uber is notifying nearly 150,000 drivers that attackers stole their data. The breach occurred after threat actors compromised AI infrastructure provider Fireworks in early October.

Barracuda links cloud breach to ARTEX AI-powered cyberattack

Data breach bleepingcomputer.com

Network and email security company Barracuda linked a breach of its cloud environment earlier this month to an AI-powered cyberattack. According to the company, the attackers gained access using a stolen cloud access key and the ARTEX AI penetration testing framework.

Fandango says hackers stole payment card details from checkout pages

Data breach bleepingcomputer.com

Ticket seller Fandango Media is notifying customers about a data breach affecting its checkout pages. According to the company, hackers stole payment card details entered by users on those pages.

N-able Patches 17 N-central Flaws as Attackers Exploit Earlier Bugs to Deploy Crypto Miners

Vulnerability Exploited Malware thehackernews.com

N-able released N-central 2026.4 Hotfix 3 as a critical security update addressing 17 additional critical flaws in its Remote Monitoring and Management and Unified Endpoint Management platform. The update comes as attackers continue to exploit earlier N-central bugs to deploy crypto miners.

Google GTIG Warns AI Agents Automate Exploits, Steal Credentials and Scale Cybercrime

Malware APT securityaffairs.com

Google's Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) warned that attackers are deploying autonomous multi-agent AI frameworks such as ARTEX, CAIRN, CyberStrikeAI, and PentAGI to automate patch reverse engineering and execute multi-stage intrusions. GTIG observed CAIRN used in real attacks against targets including US aerospace organizations, while an operator used a modified version of PentAGI between late August and early October to scan organizations across Asia. Google also found evidence that a PRC-nexus espionage group is developing its own automated attack framework.

New GitDoor and GraphDoor Backdoors Turn GitHub and SharePoint Into Covert C2 Channels

APT Malware thehackernews.com

Kaspersky disclosed an active cyber intrusion campaign codenamed Operation ShadowCloud, identified in early 2026, targeting government sectors in Asia and Africa. The campaign delivers the previously undocumented backdoors GitDoor and GraphDoor, which leverage legitimate services including Microsoft SharePoint and GitHub for command-and-control.

Ransomware affiliate suspect extradited to US, faces 27 years in prison

Law & policy Ransomware bleepingcomputer.com

A Kazakh national suspected of acting as a ransomware affiliate has been extradited from Italy to the US. He faces 27 years in prison on charges alleging he targeted US schools, hospitals, and healthcare entities in ransomware attacks.
